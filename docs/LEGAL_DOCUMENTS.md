3.1 **3-Hour Notice Rule:** In accordance with your external tuition agreement, any cancellation or request to reschedule a scheduled lesson must be made with at least **3 hours' notice** prior to the scheduled start time.  
3.2 **Late Notice:** Cancellations made with less than 3 hours' notice remain chargeable in full under your external tuition arrangement.  
3.3 **Tutor Delay Notifications:** In the event of an unavoidable delay, tutors will update the lesson status in the portal (e.g. +5m or +10m). Any time lost will be added to the session or credited accordingly.

# Document 2: Privacy Policy (UK GDPR & Data Protection Act 2018)

**Last Updated:** September 2026

### 1. Overview
LB MATHS TUITION LTD is committed to protecting the privacy of our tutors, parents, and students. This Privacy Policy sets out how personal data is handled on our **Tutor Hub** web portal in compliance with the **UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)** and the **Data Protection Act 2018**.

### 2. Strict Data Minimization: What We Collect on This Platform
Because bookings and billing are processed externally, data collection on this platform is strictly minimized:
- **Students (Minors under 18):**
  - **Student Name:** Used solely to create an individual profile and identify the student on lesson timetables.
  - **Security Credentials:** An administrator-generated 4-digit PIN and unique URL token (Magic Link) for passwordless portal entry.
  - **Lesson Records:** Timetable dates, times, attendance confirmation status, topics covered, and optional student ratings/feedback.
  - *(**Important:** We do **NOT** collect, request, or store student email addresses, home addresses, phone numbers, or dates of birth on this website).*
- **Tutors:** Full name, company-assigned email address (`@lbmathstuition.co.uk`), cryptographically hashed passwords (bcrypt), hourly pay rates, and attendance logs.
- **Technical & Security Data:** IP addresses, login timestamps, and system audit logs generated automatically to safeguard platform security and verify attendance.

### 3. Lawful Bases for Processing (UK GDPR Art. 6)
We process personal data on the platform under:
1. **Contract Performance (Art. 6(1)(b)):** To display timetables, enable access to scheduled lessons, and manage tutor/student attendance as agreed externally.
2. **Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)):** To administer the timetable securely, maintain an audit log of tuition delivered, and protect against unauthorized access.
3. **Parental Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) & Art. 8):** Parental or guardian consent obtained during external registration to establish the student's profile under their name.

### 4. Third-Party Infrastructure & Data Storage
Your data is securely stored and processed through reputable infrastructure providers:
- **Cloud Database:** **Turso (ChiselStrike Inc.)** — High-security distributed database hosted in the **AWS EU-West-1 (Europe / Ireland)** region. All platform database records remain strictly within the UK/EEA.
- **Web Hosting & Runtime:** **Deno Deploy** (Deno Land Inc.) — Serverless cloud hosting and execution.
- **Live Video Provider:** **Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited (Microsoft Teams)** — Facilitates live video calls. *No audio or video recordings are made or retained on the platform.*

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion
- **Temporary Breaks vs. Retention Erasure:** An "Inactive" status in the system represents a temporary break (e.g., summer break, holiday, or short hiatus) and does not flag a student for deletion.
- **Storage Limitation & Prolonged Inactivity Retention Policy:** In accordance with the storage limitation principle of UK GDPR (Article 5(1)(e)), student profiles that have conducted no lessons for a prolonged period (default 90 days with no future scheduled lessons) are automatically flagged for deletion on the administrator portal and queued for permanent erasure to ensure minor student personal data is never retained indefinitely.
- **Permanent Account Deletion:** When an administrator deletes a student profile from the portal, the student record, access PIN, magic links, all associated lesson history, and related audit log entries are permanently erased from our database.
- Tutors or parents may request the correction or deletion of profile details at any time by contacting `luke@lbmathstuition.co.uk`.

### 6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR
Under the UK GDPR, parents (and students) have the right to:
- Request access to any personal data held about them.
- Request the correction of inaccurate data.
- Request the erasure of personal data ("right to be forgotten").
- Object to or restrict data processing.
- Lodge a complaint with the UK supervisory authority:

This policy explains how the Tutor Hub platform uses cookies and local storage to function securely.

### 2. Strictly Necessary Cookies Only
LB MATHS TUITION LTD respects your privacy. **We do NOT use any marketing, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking cookies.**

We only use **strictly necessary technical cookies and local storage** required for authentication and interface functionality:

| Item | Type | Purpose | Expiry |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `lb_tutor_token` | First-Party Cookie (HTTP-only, Secure) | Securely authenticates logged-in tutors and administrators. | 7 days |
| `next-auth` / Portal State | First-Party Browser Local Storage | Remembers user interface state and student active session. | Session |

### 3. Exemption from Consent Banners
Under Regulation 6 of the **Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR)**, strictly necessary authentication and security cookies do not require prior opt-in consent banners.

You can manage or clear cookies through your browser settings at any time; however, clearing authentication cookies will log you out of the platform.
