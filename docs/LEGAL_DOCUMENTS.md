### 1. Overview
LB MATHS TUITION LTD is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and rights of parents, students, and tutors. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is secured, and your rights under the **UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)** and the **Data Protection Act 2018**.

### 2. What Personal Data We Collect
We practice strict data minimization. Specifically:
- **Tutors:** Full name, business/tutor email address (`@lbmathstuition.co.uk`), cryptographically hashed passwords (using bcrypt), hourly pay rates, lesson records, and confirmation logs.
- **Students (Minors under 18):** First name/display name, unique 4-digit security PIN, assigned Magic Link, assigned tutor, scheduled and completed lesson logs (date, time, duration, delay records), topics covered, and student lesson ratings/feedback.  
  *(**Note:** We do **not** collect, require, or store personal email addresses for students).*
- **Parents / Guardians:** Contact name, billing address, telephone number, and payment confirmation records.
- **System & Security Logs:** IP addresses, browser user agent, action timestamps, and audit trail logs (e.g. login events, attendance confirmations) to protect portal security.

### 3. Lawful Bases for Processing (UK GDPR Art. 6)
We process your personal data under the following lawful bases:
1. **Contract Performance (Art. 6(1)(b)):** To manage lesson scheduling, provide tuition, track attendance, and process payments.
2. **Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)):** To maintain accounting and tax records for HMRC, and to comply with statutory safeguarding obligations.
3. **Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)):** To secure our website, prevent unauthorized access, and monitor tuition service quality.
4. **Consent / Parental Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) & Art. 8):** For students under 18, parental or guardian consent is obtained to deliver tuition and process basic pupil educational details.

### 4. Third-Party Sub-Processors and Data Storage
Your data is securely processed using trusted infrastructure providers:
- **Hosting & Compute:** **Deno Deploy** (Deno Land Inc.) — Serverless execution and application delivery.
- **Cloud Database:** **Turso (ChiselStrike Inc.)** — High-security distributed database hosted in the **AWS EU-West-1 (Europe/Ireland)** region, ensuring all database records remain within the UK/EEA or compliant jurisdictions.
- **Live Video Delivery:** **Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited (Microsoft Teams)** — Provides live video/audio calling. *No video recordings are made or stored by LB MATHS TUITION LTD.*

### 5. How Long We Keep Your Data
- **Active Accounts:** Data is retained while tuition is ongoing to track educational progress.
- **Inactive / Finished Students:** If an account is deleted by an administrator, all corresponding student lesson records and associated logs are permanently purged from our database.
- **Financial & Invoice Records:** Kept for 6 full years following the relevant tax year in accordance with UK statutory accounting and HMRC requirements.

### 6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR
You (and your child) have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request deletion of personal data where it is no longer required for statutory or contractual purposes.
- **Restriction & Objection:** Object to or restrict the processing of personal data in certain circumstances.
- **Lodge a Complaint:** You have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK supervisory authority:  
  **Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO)**  
  Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF

Cookies and local browser storage are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you access websites.

| Cookie / Storage Key | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `lb_tutor_token` / Auth Cookie | First-Party (HTTP-only, Secure) | Authenticates your active login session and protects against unauthorized access. | 7 days |
| `next-auth` / Portal State | First-Party Local Storage | Remembers user interface state (e.g. selected view, theme). | Session |

### 3. Legal Exemption
Under Regulation 6 of the **Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR)** and UK GDPR, strictly necessary cookies that are essential to provide an explicitly requested service do not require prior opt-in consent banners.

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings at any time; however, disabling authentication cookies will prevent you from signing in to the platform.
